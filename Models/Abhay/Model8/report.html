Method

Three stages, 12 simulations each, separate training for both variants, 400-tree candidate scorers and 300-tree unknown detectors. Shared PCA/LDA and mutual RootSIFT evidence; unchanged unknown score rule and neighbour prediction stacking. Baseline has 15 candidates. Union has the baseline 15 plus distinct raw top-15 identities; masked padding cannot enter the learners or final predictions. Missing side: known left/right query without same-side true-identity gallery coverage. Small gallery: one to three true-identity gallery photos. No nuisance projection, submission generation or automatic baseline replacement.
