Interpretation and next step

  • Overall MAP@5 improves by 0.029297, with a conditional animal-cluster 95% bootstrap interval of [0.016626, 0.046145]. Of 1,829 query appearances, 154 improve and 46 worsen. Top-one accuracy rises from 82.94% to 87.42%.
  • Known-animal MAP@5 rises from 0.837773 to 0.850589; unknown-animal MAP@5 rises from 0.926352 to 0.975139. Unknown top-one detection rises from 86.75% to 95.23%. About 76% of the pooled MAP gain comes from unknown-query outcomes. All scoring heads were retrained, so this does not isolate the detector as the sole cause.
  • The per-episode gains are +0.039261, +0.027507 and +0.020758. Hyena MAP@5 rises from 0.878852 to 0.935677; leopard MAP@5 rises from 0.878135 to 0.895000.
  • The improvement is not uniform within species: known-hyena MAP@5 rises from 0.835981 to 0.885921, while known-leopard MAP@5 slips slightly from 0.838469 to 0.836881. Unknown results improve for both species. Known-leopard matching remains an important unresolved weakness.
  • Missing-side known-query MAP@5 improves from 0.235714 to 0.283673 across 49 appearances, and small true-identity galleries improve from 0.515050 to 0.572015 across 134 appearances. These slices are still difficult, small, and overlapping; the experiment does not establish a viewpoint-invariance fix.
  • Actual saved estimator settings and feature schemas match the unchanged baseline at all three stages. All original training queries, truths and own-identity reference photos are preserved. No validation or future-stage query identity enters an earlier fitting context. Independent reconstruction reproduces every final ranking and metric, and all 250 sampled new cross-role matches agree with the original OpenCV matcher.
  • Use this retrained stack as the preferred local candidate for the larger-gallery protocol, while retaining the original checkpoint for comparison. The trained artifact is saved separately and no competition submission has been generated.
  • Freeze this recipe and confirm it on another predeclared outer identity allocation before treating the gain as robust across retraining or claiming a leaderboard improvement. Keep evaluation animals out of all fitting contexts.
  • Prioritise the remaining known-leopard errors under this larger-gallery protocol when choosing the next matching improvement. The current gain is mainly better handling of unknown animals, rather than a general solution to weak identity evidence.
  • Keep the larger-gallery evaluation and separate known/unknown and species reporting. Query-pool and encounter composition remain smaller/different than real inference and should be the next simulation stress test, rather than tuning this arm on the current validation outcomes.

Exactly what changed

The retrained arm uses the same original 36 query episodes (12 per stage), query photos, known/unknown truths, true-identity reference photos, near-duplicate regimes, seed, 15 candidates, 150 SIFT neighbours, PCA/LDA, SIFT matcher, features, 400-tree candidate scorers, 300-tree unknown detectors, stacking and final ranking rule. Only the training galleries are enlarged with eligible background identities. The unknown detector and both candidate scorers are retrained at every stage. No hyperparameters or mixture strengths were selected on validation.

Stage 1 adds the dedicated background role (the old calibration/metric-fit role), which is never used for query targets. Stage 2 also adds all stage-1 animals; stage 3 also adds all stage-2 animals. Earlier models never see later-stage query animals, even as gallery distractors. All 110 validation animals remain excluded throughout supervised tree fitting. Episode-specific PCA/LDA still uses allowed gallery labels and unlabelled queries at evaluation, exactly as the baseline does.

The larger galleries add labelled background exposure that the frozen baseline did not receive during fitting. This is a practical larger-gallery retraining comparison, not an equal-gallery-data comparison of architectures. Stage gallery contexts deliberately overlap on background and earlier animals; query identity roles remain disjoint and future-stage identities remain excluded.

Paired validation protocol

The exact three enlarged-gallery manifests from the domain-shift analysis are reused. Both models receive identical query pools, gallery images, candidate lists, PCA/LDA and SIFT evidence. Validation unknown animals are absent from the gallery; known query animals have only their allowed reference photos. The frozen baseline reproduces all 1,829 earlier larger-gallery predictions exactly. These are 1,147 distinct photos from 105 queried identities, not 1,829 independent animals.

Predeclared promotion gate: positive overall and every-episode MAP difference, positive lower animal-cluster interval bound, no decrease in known or unknown MAP, and no decrease in unknown top-one detection. Slice outcomes are reported rather than used to retune the arm.

Limits

  • One previously inspected outer identity allocation; not an untouched test or leaderboard result.
  • Episodes reuse query photos and identities; intervals condition on fixed fitted models.
  • Background gallery labels are extra fitting exposure relative to the saved baseline.
  • Gallery growth is progressive to prevent earlier stages seeing future-stage query identities.
  • Query-pool composition is unchanged and remains smaller than real inference.

No competition test labels, submission generation or leaderboard tuning were used. Model outputs and original checkpoints are preserved separately.
